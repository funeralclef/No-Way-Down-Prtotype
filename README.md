# No Way Down

**Keep moving. Make every landing count.**

A 3D action platformer where enemies double as stepping stones. Chain stomps,
burn nitro between jumps, and build a loadout as you go. The abyss is closing in;
a good landing buys you room for the next one.

**[Play the prototype →](https://github.com/funeralclef/No-Way-Down-Prtotype/releases/latest)**

## Find your rhythm

- **Stay in the air.** Double jump, Rift through danger, and land on the next
  enemy to keep your combo going. Wind lanes and bloom pads help carry your speed.
- **Build as you run.** Clear arenas for devices and catalysts. Unlock a blink,
  a plunging strike, a homing spark, or a return anchor; merge devices with
  catalysts to evolve their abilities.
- **Pick your route.** Hunt enemy chains, follow the flow, or leave room to
  recover. Travel through ruins, forests, snow, neon rain, ash, and floating islands.
- **Earn the finish.** Face one of three guardians at the fourth arena.
  Beat it, then finish the run or keep going in endless play.

## Play

Get the **[latest release](https://github.com/funeralclef/No-Way-Down-Prtotype/releases/latest)**:

| Platform | Start playing |
| --- | --- |
| Windows x64 | Extract the ZIP and run `NoWayDown.exe`. Keep its data folder alongside it. |
| Linux x64 | Extract the tar.gz and run `NoWayDown.x86_64`. |
| Android ARM64 | Install the APK. Requires Android 7.0 or later. |

Keyboard, controller, and touch controls. Open **INFO** in-game for the guide.

**Prototype 0.1.0 · English only.** Expect rough edges; feedback is welcome in
[Issues](https://github.com/funeralclef/No-Way-Down-Prtotype/issues).
